Academies

A pathway for every player.

From a child's first swing to representing the UAE on the national team — inclusive academies for men, women and juniors, designed to build skill, confidence and community at every level.

Ladies Academy

One of the most successful programs in Abu Dhabi, empowering women players through tailored coaching and community events. Two courts are frequently reserved for ladies-only sessions, offering a comfortable, inclusive environment to train, compete and connect.

Led by dedicated Ladies Academy coaches, the program takes players from their very first lesson through to competitive club play — building genuine community along the way.

Kids & Junior Academy

Investing in children's development is at the heart of what we do. Our Junior Academy — led by a dedicated Kids Academy Manager — introduces young players to padel in a safe, structured and genuinely fun environment, building fundamentals, coordination and a lifelong love of the sport.

As juniors progress, we provide a clear pathway from first swing to competitive play — with age-appropriate coaching, friendly match play, and the same internationally certified coaching standards used across every Padel Beast academy.

We're also proud to open the game to UAE locals and Emirati families, reflecting Abu Dhabi's vision of active, healthy youth and positioning the emirate as a regional sports hub.
