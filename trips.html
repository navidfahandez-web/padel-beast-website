Trip recap

Mallorca, June 2026.

A week based in Port de Sóller — padel training with our coaches, a private chef, and games against the locals at Tennis i Pàdel Sóller Club. We stayed at Hotel Marina with breakfast included, evenings were left open for group dinners, and we took a free day to explore Sóller's local market and take a boat trip along the coast.
