Who we are

We specialize in building vibrant padel ecosystems.

Padel Beast is a community-first padel operator based in Abu Dhabi. From coaching to fitness to events, we transform venues into thriving communities.

We're proudly Abu Dhabi–grown — founded on the belief that clubs don't succeed on courts alone. Culture and loyalty are what keep players coming back, so we build the ecosystem around the game: coaching pathways, fitness programs, community events and a matchmaking model tuned by real coaches, not just an algorithm.

Padel Beast is more than a service — it's a lifestyle. We create an inclusive culture where people of all ages, backgrounds and skill levels feel connected, motivated, and part of something bigger.

"We don't just fill courts. We build communities. That's the difference between a club people visit once, and a club they never leave."

Navid Fahandezh

CEO & Co-Founder, Padel Beast

Player achievement

Bassam Al Khatib — 6,000 meters, flag in hand.

Padel Beast player Bassam Al Khatib took our flag with him on an attempt at one of the world's highest mountains, planting a Padel Beast t-shirt at 6,000 meters above sea level.

"When everything got quiet up there, when the altitude was pressing down and the body was starting to betray me, you were all in my head. The court, the battles, the brotherhood we've built. I reached into my pack and pulled out the Padel Beast flag and I planted it at 6,000 meters."

"That's what this community means to me. You don't need the summit to prove you belong on the mountain. You push until the mountain speaks and then you listen."

— Bassam Al Khatib
